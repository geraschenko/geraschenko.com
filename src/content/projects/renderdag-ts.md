My favorite text renderer for DAGs is Meta's [sapling-renderdag](https://lib.rs/crates/sapling-renderdag). This is a dependency-free TypeScript port of it. I use it to make nice diagrams of [VCS history](blog/review-commands) and [session files](blog/claude-context), and it powers clauctl's `/tree` view.
