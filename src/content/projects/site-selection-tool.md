---
title: Verily Site Selection Tool
url: https://github.com/verilylifesciences/site-selection-tool
date: 2020-10-14
---

A differentiable Monte Carlo simulator of a phase 3 clinical trial, built to help a pharma partner plan a COVID vaccine trial: the planner states the forecasts they believe, sees how participant recruitment strategies affect time to success, and can use gradient-based optimization to guide decisions.
